About the Team

We're a group of passionate high schoolers working to advance our fellow students' STEM knowledge, spread our love of STEM, and build the best robot we possibly can.

Team Spectre #36363 competes in FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC), an international robotics program where teams of students design, build, program, and drive a robot to compete in a new head-to-head game announced every season. Alongside the build season, teams are judged on engineering practices, teamwork, and community outreach.

01

Gracious Professionalism

We compete hard while treating every opponent, volunteer, and mentor with respect and kindness.

02

Iterative Engineering

We prototype fast, test often, and aren't afraid to rebuild something that isn't working.

03

STEM Outreach

We run workshops and demos that get younger students excited about robotics and STEM.

04

Team Spirit

We come from a variety of teams who grew to respect each other's craftsmanship on the field, and came together to form Spectre.
